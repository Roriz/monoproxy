> Updated 2026-10-18: EPUB wording replaced with current copy; see the EPUB copy rule in docs/enrichreader-seo.md.

The page keeps its existing layout, product scope, guide availability, account
requirements, and privacy distinctions. The wording is aimed at readers who
want help remembering a story, so the closing invitation avoids suggesting that
they should become more lost.

## What still sounded generated in the draft

- The closing “Spend less time searching. Enjoy the next chapter.” still uses a
  manufactured two-part marketing rhythm. The final uses one invitation.
- The profile description and boundary label still read as fragments. The final
  names the actor and explains what the profile shows.
- The hero's trailing “showing only...” clause repeats the mechanism indirectly.
  The final states the reading-position limit in the main sentence.
