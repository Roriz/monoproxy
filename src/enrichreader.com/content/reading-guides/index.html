Reading guides that stop where you stop

An EnrichReader web guide is a chapter-aware companion to your book. Choose your current letter or chapter to see character profiles, places, and story context from the sections you have already finished. You can keep reading your own edition without importing a file or installing an app.

How to choose your position

Choose the section you are currently reading. The guide shows information from the sections before it, so you can look things up before finishing the current chapter. After you finish it, move to the next section. Select “Terminei este livro” only when you have finished the book and are comfortable seeing its full story.

Start with Frankenstein, by Mary Shelley

The published Frankenstein guide follows the 1831 text in the Standard Ebooks edition: four opening letters followed by 24 chapters. Its interface is in Portuguese and its character profiles are in English. In the position selector, “Carta” means letter and “Cap.” means chapter. If your edition numbers sections differently, match the section before relying on a lookup.

A lookup example from the opening letter

In Letter 1, R. Walton is an English explorer preparing an expedition to the North Pole and writing to his sister. After you finish that letter, choose “Carta 2” to consult what Letter 1 has established. Later information stays outside that lookup until you advance your position. You can open the Frankenstein guide and choose your own position; this link does not preselect a later chapter.

Where the information comes from

EnrichReader prepares guide data from the source text with AI assistance. Facts show their source chapter, and the guide offers “Algo errado?” to report a mistake. Frankenstein is a public-domain example; it does not imply approval by Mary Shelley. Guide data can contain errors, so use the cited passage to check a detail that seems inconsistent with your reading.

Web guide or app?

A web guide sits alongside a book you are reading elsewhere. The EnrichReader app opens your own DRM-free books and adds companion features when a matching Skin is available. A published web guide does not mean its Skin is available in the app: check the app’s catalog separately.
