1. How We Handle Your Books

We believe your library belongs to you.

  • Strictly Local Access: When you open an EPUB file in EnrichReader, the file remains stored locally on your device. We never upload the text, content, or binary files of your books to our servers.

  • Zero Broad Storage Permissions: EnrichReader operates exclusively through native operating system document pickers (Android Storage Access Framework / SAF and your browser's file picker). We do not request or require broad filesystem access permissions (such as Android's READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). File access is strictly limited to the specific EPUB files you deliberately choose to open within our sandboxed application storage.

  • Local EPUB File Preservation: Your original EPUB files stored in your device folders (such as Downloads, Documents, or external storage) remain under your exclusive control. EnrichReader never alters, deletes, or moves your original EPUB files on disk. If you uninstall the App or delete your account, your local book files remain completely untouched and intact in their original locations on your device.

  • The Match Process: To match your books with our interactive "Skins" (structured narrative metadata packages comprising character registries, relationship graphs, timelines, and non-spoiler entity guides), the App generates a numerical "fingerprint" of text segments locally on your device.

    • • These hashes are non-reversible (they cannot be turned back into text).
    • • Hash data is matched locally; no text fingerprint is sent to our servers.

  • Telemetry & Crash Log Sanitization: Application diagnostics (Rollbar) feature automated client-side scrubbing. File system paths containing book titles, EPUB file names, book text, and reader annotations are filtered and stripped before error logs leave your device.

  • Ephemeral Parsing & Sandboxed Storage: When rendering an EPUB, content is parsed into transient, volatile device memory (RAM) or an ephemeral sandboxed application cache. We never write unencrypted book text or decrypted chapters to persistent non-sandboxed storage. All temporary parsing buffers are expunged automatically when the reading session ends or the App is closed. Downloaded narrative Skins are stored in sandboxed offline cache and can be manually cleared anytime in Settings → Storage & Cache → Clear Skin Cache.

7. Storage, Security & Data Retention

Location & Infrastructure

Account and sync metadata records are stored on dedicated, self-hosted PostgreSQL database servers operated directly by ENRICH DESENVOLVIMENTO DE SOFTWARES LTDA., secured with granular Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring logical isolation and strict tenant access boundaries without reliance on third-party database-as-a-service providers.

Encryption & Keystore Protection

We enforce industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit and AES-256 cryptographic standards at rest. On your mobile device, session tokens and authentication credentials are encrypted using hardware-backed security modules (Android Keystore). Tokens are never written to plaintext shared preferences and are immediately revoked and wiped upon sign-out.

Security Incident & Breach Notification Protocol

We maintain rigorous incident response protocols. In the unlikely event of a security incident or personal data breach that poses a potential risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects:

  • GDPR Compliance (EU/EEA/UK): We will notify the competent supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, pursuant to GDPR Article 33. High-risk incidents will be communicated to affected users without undue delay (GDPR Article 34).
  • LGPD Compliance (Brazil): Pursuant to LGPD Article 48 and ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024, we will notify the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) and affected data subjects within 3 (three) business days from the moment we become aware of a security incident that may create relevant risk or damage.

Data Retention Schedules

  • Account Profile & Preferences: Retained for the active lifetime of your account. Upon account deletion in-app or via verified request, all personal records and sync markers are purged or anonymized within 30 days.
  • Feedback-Email Answers & Sign-Up Source: We keep these while you have an account, because they show what you agreed to and when. We delete them when your account is deleted.
  • Application Access Logs (Marco Civil da Internet): Pursuant to Article 15 of Brazilian Federal Law No. 12,965/2014 (Marco Civil da Internet), basic internet application access records (connection timestamps, IP addresses, and session identifiers) are stored securely under strict cryptographic access controls for the mandatory statutory period of 6 (six) months, after which they are permanently expunged.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs (Rollbar): Scrubbed technical diagnostics are retained for a maximum rolling window of 90 days, after which they are automatically expunged.
  • Telemetry & Analytics (Google Analytics): Aggregated event and interaction data is retained for a maximum rolling period of 14 months.
  • Zero Financial or Billing Records: Because EnrichReader and its cloud synchronization are 100% free of charge with zero paid subscriptions or in-app paywalls, we collect, process, and retain zero billing records, credit card numbers, payment tokens, or tax invoices.
  • Skin Source Material: Source text approved for Skin generation (public domain, openly licensed, or covered by a rights-holder partnership or permission) is held for a maximum of 30 days for processing and quality inspection, then permanently destroyed.
  • Technical Erasure & Disaster Recovery Backup Purge Lifecycle (5-Stage Protocol)

    Pursuant to GDPR Article 17, LGPD Article 18, VI, and established supervisory authority guidance (EDPB, CNIL & UK ICO), live production databases and disaster recovery archives operate under a rigorous, 5-stage technical purge and suppression protocol:

    Stage 1 • Immediate Production Suppression (T + 0)

    Upon receiving an in-app or verified web deletion request, authentication tokens are immediately revoked, user sessions are invalidated across all active devices, and cloud sync endpoints are severed. The account is flagged as tombstoned and rendered inaccessible to operational queries.

    Stage 2 • Production Hard Erasure (Within 30 Days)

    All personal identifiers, email records, and numeric reading progress pointers are permanently purged or cryptographically sanitized from active self-hosted PostgreSQL database tables, complying with the 30-day statutory fulfillment standard.

    Stage 3 • Immutable WAL Archives & PITR Overwrite (Max 30-Day Rotation)

    Encrypted continuous Write-Ahead Logs (WAL) and Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR) physical block-level snapshots are retained strictly for catastrophic disaster recovery in isolated cold storage. These immutable snapshots are mathematically overwritten and expunged on an automated rolling lifecycle of up to 30 days.

    Stage 4 • Restoration Suppression Protocol ("Tombstone Registry")

    In the catastrophic event that database restoration from an immutable snapshot created prior to an erasure request is legally required to restore service integrity, a dedicated, persistent cryptographic Tombstone Registry automatically re-applies all pending erasure orders, ensuring deleted accounts are immediately re-purged before live traffic resumes.

    Stage 5 • Client-Side Operating System Cloud Backups

    Device-level backups performed by mobile operating systems (such as Google Drive / Android Backup) are governed entirely by your personal operating system settings and cloud vendor agreements. EnrichReader does not possess administrative access to your personal cloud backups; users can manage or expunge application container backups directly through Android storage settings.
