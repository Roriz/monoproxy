Your books stay on your device

Your books are stored and opened on your device. Their content never reaches our servers and is never shared. Accounts are optional for reading and required for Skins and sync. See our privacy policy for usage data, crash reports, and controls.

Before you start

Is EnrichReader free?

Yes, currently. Reading, Skins and sync are free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers, as set out in the current reader terms.

Which books can I open?

Books you own, obtained lawfully and free of DRM. Compatibility can vary by edition. EnrichReader does not sell or supply books.

What if my story has no Skin?

You can still read it normally, without character profiles or atmosphere. Check the in-app catalog, or request a companion from the app; requests have no promised delivery date. To try a Skin first, open the included sample book, Initiation Protocol.

Does it spoil later chapters?

With a matching Skin, profiles and lore are limited to the furthest chapter you’ve reached. Spoiler settings let you widen that, for example for a reread. Companion data can contain mistakes; report anything that shows up too early. See how EnrichReader works for details.
