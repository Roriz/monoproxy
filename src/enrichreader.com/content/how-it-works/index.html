How EnrichReader works

It only knows
what you've read.

EnrichReader keeps track of where you are in the story and shows characters, places and lore up to that point, never past it. Here's how, from the book you bring to the name you tap.

01 The one rule

Nothing past
your chapter.

Everything EnrichReader knows about a story is stored as small facts, and each fact is tagged with the chapter that reveals it. When you open a profile, the app shows the facts from chapters you've read and holds back the rest.

It remembers the furthest chapter you've reached. If you go back to reread an early scene, what you've already seen stays visible: what's revealed stays revealed. And if the app can't tell where you are, it shows nothing rather than guessing.

03 In the app

From your book
to the first name you tap.

  1. 1

    Bring your book

    Import a DRM-free book file you own. It's stored and opened on your device, and you don't need an account to read it. The reader works with or without a Skin.

  2. 2

    Find its Skin

    A Skin is the companion for one story: its characters, places, organizations and lore, with every fact tagged by chapter. Skins are prepared ahead of time for partner series. Sign in and search the catalog; if your book isn't there yet, ask for it and the app will tell you when it's ready.

  3. 3

    Match it to your copy

    The first time you open the book, the app lines the Skin up with your own file, chapter by chapter, right on your device. It takes a couple of minutes, once. If your edition is too different to line up, the app says so instead of guessing.

  4. 4

    Read, and tap a name

    Names the Skin knows glow in the text. Tap one to see who they are, as of the chapter you're in. The first time someone appears, the app tells you their details will unlock as the story goes on.

Want to see it before importing anything? The app comes with a sample book, Initiation Protocol, already matched to its Skin, and a short tour.

04 While you read

A companion that
reads along with you.

Profiles that grow

Each profile shows the chapter it's up to date with. When a chapter changes what you know about someone, the profile shows what that chapter revealed.

Appearances so far

A timeline marks the chapters where a character shows up. Chapters you haven't read stay locked; you can tap to peek, and the app asks first.

The Codex

Everyone and everything you've met, in one place, with what's new since you last read. Search only finds people you've already encountered.

Welcome back

Coming back after a break? A briefing shows where you are, how long it's been, and what was going on: the arc and the leads still open. It appears when the Skin includes story data.

Atmosphere

Weather and light play behind the text, and artwork marks key scenes, each appearing when you reach that moment. What you see depends on the Skin; you can turn the effects off in settings.

Something look wrong?

Every profile has a way to report a mistake. A report sends that chapter's text, the titles and your note to us, so we can fix the Skin.

05 How a Skin is made

Built from the book,
before you open it.

Skins are made ahead of time for partner series, from the book's text, by EnrichReader's own AI model, which runs on our own hardware. Every fact it records is tagged with the chapter that reveals it, so the app can hold it back until you get there.

Your own book is never sent to it. A Skin can still contain mistakes; when you report one, we fix the Skin.

06 The spoiler shield

You decide how
careful it is.

Set a default for every book, or change it for just one. Anything looser than what you've read asks before it shows you more.

Strict
Only what you've read, up to your chapter.
Volume
Everything up to the end of the volume you're in. Handy when you've already read ahead somewhere else.
Off
Everything the Skin knows. For rereads, or when you've finished the series.

07 In the browser

Reading guides:
you pick the chapter.

A reading guide works next to a book you're reading anywhere: paper, another app, a library copy. Choose the letter or chapter you're on, and the guide shows characters, places and story context from the sections you've finished. Nothing to install, no account, and no book to import.

Your position lives in the link, so a guide you bookmark opens where you left it. When you finish a section, move the picker forward. The first guide is Mary Shelley's Frankenstein.

08 The fine print

Good to know.

Books, accounts, privacy and what it costs.

Your book
The reader opens DRM-free book files you own. EnrichReader doesn't sell or provide books, and companion features need a Skin for your story.
Privacy
Imported books stay on your device. Sync covers your library details, reading positions and settings, not book text. Reporting a chapter problem sends that chapter's text. The privacy policy has the details.
Accounts
Reading needs no account. Sign in to install Skins and to sync between devices.
Offline
Once your book and its Skin are on your device, you can read offline. Installing a Skin needs a connection.
Cost
The app is free to download, with no in-app purchases.
