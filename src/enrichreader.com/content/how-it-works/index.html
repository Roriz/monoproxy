How EnrichReader works

It only knows
what you've read.

With a matching Skin installed, EnrichReader follows your reading progress and limits character profiles, places and lore to that point. Chapter references help you check what you’re seeing.

01 The one rule

Nothing past
your chapter.

EnrichReader stores what it knows about a story as small facts, and each one is tagged with the chapter that reveals it. Open a profile and you get the facts from chapters you've read. The rest stays hidden.

The app remembers the furthest chapter you've reached, so going back to reread an early scene doesn't hide anything you've already seen. If it can't work out where you are, it shows you nothing at all.

In the app, or beside your book.

03 In the app

Four steps,
then you're reading.

  1. 1

    Bring your book

    Import your lawfully obtained, DRM-free EPUB, PDF or TXT file. Compatibility can vary by edition and PDF layout. The app opens it on your device, and ordinary reading works without an account or a Skin.

  2. 2

    Find its Skin

    A Skin is the companion for one story: its characters, places, organizations and lore, each fact tagged with a chapter. We prepare Skins ahead of time for supported stories. Sign in and search the catalog. If your book isn't there, you can request a companion in the app. Requests have no promised delivery date.

  3. 3

    Match it to your copy

    The first time you open the book, the app lines the Skin up with your file, chapter by chapter, on your device. That takes a couple of minutes and only happens once. If your edition is too different to line up, the app tells you rather than matching the wrong chapters.

  4. 4

    Read, and tap a name

    Names the Skin knows glow in the text. Tap one to see who they are as of the chapter you're in. When someone shows up for the first time, the app tells you their details unlock in later chapters.

If you'd rather try it before importing anything, the app comes with a sample book, Initiation Protocol, already matched to its Skin, plus a short tour.

04 While you read

A companion that
reads along with you.

Profiles that grow

Each profile tells you which chapter it's current as of. When a chapter changes what you know about someone, you can see exactly what that chapter added.

Appearances so far

A timeline marks the chapters where a character shows up. The ones you haven't read stay locked. You can tap to peek, but the app checks with you first.

The Codex

The Codex collects everyone and everything you've met and flags what's new since your last session. Its search only finds people you've already run into.

Welcome back

If you put the book down for a while, a briefing tells you where you are, how long it's been and what was going on, including the current arc and any leads still open. It shows up when the Skin has story data.

Atmosphere

Weather and light play behind the text, and artwork shows up at key scenes when you reach them. It depends on the Skin, and you can switch the effects off in settings.

Something look wrong?

Every profile lets you report a mistake. The report sends us that chapter's text, the titles and your note, and we fix the Skin.

05 How a Skin is made

Built from the book,
before you open it.

Skins are prepared ahead of time for supported stories. Facts include chapter references so the app can show context according to your reading progress.

Importing a book does not create a Skin. Companion data can contain errors; use the report option to flag a mistake.

06 The spoiler shield

You decide how
careful it is.

Pick a default for all your books, or change it for one. If you choose something looser than where you are, the app asks before showing you more.

Strict
Only what you've read, up to your chapter.
Volume
Everything up to the end of the volume you're in. Useful if you've already read ahead somewhere else.
Off
Everything the Skin knows, for rereads or once you've finished the series.

07 In the browser

Reading guides:
you pick the chapter.

A reading guide sits next to whatever you're reading, whether that's a paperback, another app or a library copy. Choose the letter or chapter you're on, and the guide shows characters, places and story context from the sections you've finished. You don't need to install anything or sign up.

The link remembers your position, so a bookmarked guide opens where you left off. When you finish a section, move the picker forward. The first guide is Mary Shelley's Frankenstein.

08 The fine print

Good to know.

Books, accounts, privacy and cost.

Your book
The reader opens DRM-free book files you own. EnrichReader doesn't sell books, and you need a Skin for your story to get the companion features.
Privacy
Imported books stay on your device. Sync covers your library, reading positions and settings, but never book text. Reporting a chapter problem does send that chapter's text. The privacy policy has the details.
Accounts
You can read without an account. You need one to install Skins and to sync between devices.
Offline
Once your book and its Skin are on your device, you can read offline. You need a connection to install a Skin.
Cost
Reading, Skins and sync are currently free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers. Current reader terms.
