EnrichReader is a reading app with a story companion built in. Companion features work in stories with a matching Skin; check the app’s catalog for yours.

What is a story companion?

A story companion is a reference for one book or series that only knows what you’ve read. A wiki describes a character using the whole story. A companion describes them as of your current chapter: who they are, where you met them and what has changed since. In EnrichReader, a story’s companion is called a Skin. It is prepared in advance for that story, and every fact in it is tagged with the chapter that reveals it.

A familiar name, without the wiki detour

A character returns after a hundred chapters, and you can’t quite place them. With a matching Skin installed, tap their name in EnrichReader to open a profile tied to your reading progress. Check what you already know, then return to the page.

Let the scene set the mood

Supported Skins can add weather, light and artwork as you reach a scene. Atmosphere depends on the Skin and can be switched off in settings. Character profiles and atmosphere bring context into the reading experience itself.

Coming back after a break

Long series are easy to put down and hard to pick up again. Open the book at your saved position and the Codex lists everyone and everything you’ve met, with what’s new since your last session flagged. When the Skin includes story data, a briefing also recaps where you are, the current arc and any leads still open. Everything stops at the chapter you’ve reached.

Story companion, wiki or re-reading?

A fan wiki covers more series and is the better reference once you’ve finished, but its pages are written from the whole story. Re-reading earlier chapters is safe and slow. Asking a community works when someone answers without spoiling. A companion is quickest when it exists for your story, and it is limited to stories with a matching Skin.

Find your story’s companion

A Skin covers one story’s characters, places, organizations and lore, with chapter references. Check the in-app catalog for your story and edition. Importing a book does not create a Skin; ordinary reading works without one. You can request a missing companion in the app, with no promised delivery date.

Try a story with its companion

The app includes Initiation Protocol, a sample book with a matched Skin and a short tour. Try a character lookup before importing your own book. To see the same idea in a browser first, open a free reading guide, such as the one for Mary Shelley’s Frankenstein.

Story companion FAQ

Which stories have a companion?

Those with a Skin in the app’s catalog. Coverage is not universal and changes over time, so check the catalog for your story and edition rather than relying on a list here.

Is it completely spoiler-free?

It is designed to show only what you’ve reached. The app follows the furthest chapter you’ve read, and you can set the spoiler shield to Strict, Volume or Off. Companion data can still contain errors, including unintended spoilers. You can report a mistake from any profile.

Do I need my own book?

Yes. EnrichReader uses your own lawfully obtained, DRM-free copy. A Skin is separate companion data, not the book.

Which formats can I import?

EPUB, PDF and TXT. Compatibility can vary by edition and PDF layout; a supported file does not guarantee a matching Skin.

What does it cost?

Reading, Skins and sync are currently free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers.

Do I need an account?

Ordinary reading needs no account. Sign in to install Skins or sync your library details, reading positions and settings.

Are my books uploaded?

Imported books stay on your device. Ordinary reading and sync do not upload book text. If you submit a chapter-problem report, it includes that chapter’s text.
