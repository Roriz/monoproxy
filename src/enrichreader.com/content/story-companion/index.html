Remember the cast. Stay in the story.

Read with character profiles and atmosphere that follow your progress in supported stories. Check the app’s Skin catalog for your story.

Open EnrichReader

A familiar name, without the wiki detour

A character returns after a hundred chapters, and you can’t quite place them. With a matching Skin installed, tap their name in EnrichReader to open a profile tied to your reading progress. Check what you already know, then return to the page.

Let the scene set the mood

Supported Skins can add weather, light and artwork as you reach a scene. Atmosphere depends on the Skin and can be switched off in settings. Character profiles and atmosphere bring context into the reading experience itself.

Find your story’s companion

A Skin is companion data prepared in advance for one story. It adds character profiles and lore with chapter references. Check the in-app catalog for your story and edition. Importing a book does not create a Skin; ordinary reading works without one. You can request a missing companion in the app, with no promised delivery date.

Choose where to read

Read in the Android app or the web reader. On iPhone and iPad, add the web app to your Home Screen from Safari. Once your book and any matching Skin are downloaded, you can read offline.

Try a story with its companion

The app includes Initiation Protocol, a sample book with a matched Skin and a short tour. Try a character lookup before importing your own book. For other stories, check the catalog for a matching Skin.

Before you start

Do I need my own book?

Yes. EnrichReader uses your own lawfully obtained, DRM-free copy. A Skin is separate companion data, not the book.

Which formats can I import?

EPUB, PDF and TXT. Compatibility can vary by edition and PDF layout; a supported file does not guarantee a matching Skin.

What does it cost?

Reading, Skins and sync are currently free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers.

Do I need an account?

Ordinary reading needs no account. Sign in to install Skins or sync your library details, reading positions and settings.

Are my books uploaded?

Imported books stay on your device. Ordinary reading and sync do not upload book text. If you submit a chapter-problem report, it includes that chapter’s text.
