A spoiler-free wiki alternative for supported stories

Fan wikis describe characters with knowledge from the whole story, so a quick lookup mid-book can reveal identities, deaths or betrayals you haven’t reached. A spoiler-free alternative limits what you see to your current chapter. EnrichReader does this inside the reader for stories with a matching Skin, and in free browser guides for the books that have one.

How to look up a character without spoilers

  • Know your chapter. Note exactly where you are before you search, especially after a break or a switch between editions.
  • Search your own book first. An ebook search for the name shows earlier mentions. Open only the matches before your current page.
  • Open a wiki with care. Skip the summary box and opening lines, which often name later roles, and read only the sections for arcs you’ve finished.
  • Ask with your position. In a community thread, say which chapter you’re on and ask for spoiler tags.
  • Use a companion limited to your chapter when one exists for your story.

How progress changes a character lookup

A wiki article usually describes a character using knowledge from across the story. That is useful after finishing, but it can expose later identities, relationships, or outcomes during a first read. EnrichReader uses your reading position to limit the information in a matching Skin. Profiles, lore entries, and relevant artwork become available as you reach them. Check that your position is correct before consulting the companion, especially if you have moved between editions or returned after a break.

Wiki alternative FAQ

Is there a wiki without spoilers?

Fan wikis are usually kept up to date with the latest chapter, so their articles assume you know the plot so far. A chapter-limited companion is the closest alternative: it shows what the story has revealed up to your position and nothing after.

Is EnrichReader a wiki?

No. It is a reading app. Its companion data is prepared by EnrichReader for each supported story, not edited by readers, and you can report a mistake from any profile.

Can it replace a wiki completely?

No. A wiki covers more series and more detail, and it is a good reference once you’ve finished. The companion is for the lookups you make while you are still reading.

Does it work for my series?

Check the Skin catalog in the app for your story and edition. If there is no match, ordinary reading still works, and you can request a companion in the app, with no promised delivery date.

Can a lookup still spoil something?

Yes, it can happen. Companion data can contain errors, including unintended spoilers, and a wrong saved position shows the wrong information. Check your position, edition match and spoiler settings before a lookup.
