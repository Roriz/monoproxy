A spoiler-free wiki alternative for supported stories

Open EnrichReader

Answer “who is this?” at your current chapter

EnrichReader is a spoiler-free story companion, with character and lore Skins that follow your reading progress. For a partner series with a matching Skin, it offers an alternative to opening a fan wiki whenever you forget a name. A character profile shows information up to your current position in the book. This makes the lookup useful while you are still reading, when even a later title or an unexpected alias can give too much away.

How progress changes a character lookup

A wiki article usually describes a character using knowledge from across the story. That is useful after finishing, but it can expose later identities, relationships, or outcomes during a first read. EnrichReader uses your reading position to limit the information in a matching Skin. Profiles, lore entries, and relevant artwork become available as you reach them. Check that your position is correct before consulting the companion, especially if you have moved between editions or returned after a break.

What you need to use the companion

Bring your own lawfully obtained, DRM-free book file and check the app’s Skin catalog for a matching companion. Skins are prepared in advance for supported works. Importing a book does not automatically generate profiles, and coverage is not universal. You can read your books without an account, but installing Skins requires sign-in. The story companion guide explains local reading and platform choices; the launch page links to Android and the web app, including iOS Home Screen installation.

Refresh your memory after time away

A long web novel can be hard to resume when you have forgotten a side character or a faction. With a matching Skin installed, return to your saved position and consult the profiles and directory there. You can refresh the context you have already reached while keeping later information out of that lookup. This is the same reason the companion can help with LitRPG and progression fantasy: a large cast makes remembering every introduction difficult over hundreds of chapters.
