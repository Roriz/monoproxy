About EnrichReader

EnrichReader is a reading app for long stories, with character profiles and lore that follow your progress in supported books.

Remember the cast. Stay in the story.

Start reading

What it does

You read your own book in EnrichReader. When the story has a matching Skin, you can tap a character’s name to see who they are as of the chapter you’ve reached, and nothing after it. The same goes for places, organizations and lore. Some Skins also add atmosphere: weather, light and artwork at key scenes.

A Skin is the companion data for one story, prepared in advance, with every fact tagged to the chapter that reveals it. Without a Skin, the app is still an ordinary reader.

Who makes it

EnrichReader was founded by Radamés Roriz. He built it because keeping up with the cast of a long series had turned into memory work. In his words: “keeping track of 50 characters over 1,400 chapters started feeling like homework.” The app is meant to handle that part, so you can keep reading.

Where it stands

The app is live and has had early readers for months. It is still being developed, so features and the list of supported stories change over time. The catalog inside the app is the place to check what is covered today.

Where to read

There is an Android app on Google Play and a web reader that runs in the browser, on desktop too. There is no iOS app in the App Store: on iPhone and iPad, open the web reader in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. Once your book and its Skin are on your device, you can read offline.

What it costs

Reading, Skins and sync are currently free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers. That is the current policy in the reader terms, not a permanent pricing promise. You can read without an account; you need one to install Skins and to sync between devices.

Which stories it covers

Companion features need a Skin for your story, and coverage is not universal. Check the in-app catalog for your story and edition. If it isn’t there, you can request a companion in the app; requests have no promised delivery date.

The free reading guides on this site use public-domain books. A book still under copyright can get an official guide only with its author’s approval. A guide on this site and a Skin in the app are separate, so one doesn’t imply the other.

Privacy, in one sentence

Books you import stay on your device and sync never includes book text, with one exception: if you report a problem with a chapter, the report sends us that chapter’s text, its titles and your note.

Privacy policy

What EnrichReader is not

Not a bookstore

It doesn’t sell or supply books. You read your own lawfully obtained, DRM-free copy.

Not affiliated with the series it mentions

EnrichReader is independent. Books, characters and worlds named on this site belong to their authors and publishers, and naming a series doesn’t mean its author endorses EnrichReader.

Not a live AI summary of your file

Skins are prepared ahead of time for supported stories. Importing a book doesn’t create one, and the book you import isn’t used to make one.

Not a fan wiki

Readers don’t edit it, and by default it only shows what the story has told you so far. A wiki covers more and is the better reference once you’ve finished.
