EnrichReader, explained

What is a Skin?

A Skin is the companion data EnrichReader prepares in advance for one specific story: character profiles, places, organizations and lore, with each fact tagged to the chapter that reveals it. With a matching Skin installed, the app shows you only the facts from chapters you’ve reached.

A Skin is not the book. You read your own copy, and the Skin sits alongside it.

Start reading

What’s inside a Skin

Every Skin covers one story. What it holds is broken into small facts, and each fact records the chapter where the story reveals it.

Character profiles

Tap a name in the text to see who that person is as of your chapter. A profile says which chapter it is current as of, shows what each new chapter added, and marks the chapters where the character appears.

Places and organizations

Places, factions and other groups get their own entries, limited to your chapter in the same way.

Lore

How the world works, as far as the story has explained it so far.

The Codex

One list of everyone and everything you’ve met, with what’s new since your last session flagged. Its search only finds what you’ve already come across.

Atmosphere

Some Skins add weather and light behind the text and artwork at key scenes. It depends on the Skin, and you can switch the effects off in settings.

How a Skin follows your reading

The app tracks the furthest chapter you’ve reached and shows facts up to that point. Going back to reread an early scene doesn’t hide anything you’ve already seen. If the app can’t work out where you are, it shows nothing.

The spoiler shield decides how careful it is. You can set a default for all your books or change it for one. If you pick something looser than where you are, the app asks before showing more.

Strict

Only what you’ve read, up to your chapter.

Volume

Everything up to the end of the volume you’re in. Useful if you’ve already read ahead somewhere else.

Off

Everything the Skin knows, for rereads or once you’ve finished the series.

Matching a Skin to your edition

The first time you open a book with its Skin, the app lines the two up chapter by chapter, on your device. It takes a couple of minutes and happens once. Editions differ, so check the catalog for your story and edition. If your copy is too different to line up, the app tells you instead of matching the wrong chapters.

Coming back after a break

When a Skin includes story data, a welcome-back briefing appears after you’ve been away for a while. It tells you where you are, how long it’s been and what was going on, including the current arc and any leads still open. Like everything else in the Skin, it stops at your chapter.

If your story has no Skin

You can still read the book normally, just without profiles or atmosphere. Importing a book doesn’t create a Skin. You can request a companion for your story in the app; requests have no promised delivery date.

When a Skin gets something wrong

Skins can contain mistakes, including a fact that shows up too early. Every profile has a report option, so you can tell us about a mistake and we can correct the Skin.

Skins and reading guides

The reading guides on this site follow the same chapter rule, but you choose the chapter yourself and they don’t need the app. A guide is not a Skin: a guide for a book doesn’t mean the app has a Skin for it. To try a Skin without importing anything, open the sample book in the app, Initiation Protocol, which comes matched to its Skin.

Skin FAQ

What is a Skin in EnrichReader?

A Skin is the companion for one story: character profiles, places, organizations and lore, prepared in advance, with each fact tagged to the chapter that reveals it. The app uses it to show only what you’ve reached.

Does a Skin include the book?

No. You bring your own lawfully obtained, DRM-free copy of the book. The Skin is separate companion data that the app lines up with your copy.

Is a Skin made from my file?

No. Skins are prepared ahead of time for supported stories. Importing a book doesn’t create one. Your book stays on your device: its content never reaches our servers and is never shared.

Can a Skin spoil the story?

It is designed to show only what you’ve reached, but a Skin can contain mistakes, and a wrong saved position or a looser spoiler setting shows more. Report anything that appears too early from the profile.

Which stories have a Skin?

Those listed in the app’s catalog. Coverage changes over time, so check the catalog for your story and edition. If yours is missing, request it in the app.

Do Skins cost anything?

Not at the moment. Reading, Skins and sync are currently free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers. You need an account to install a Skin.

Can I use a Skin offline?

Yes, once it is installed. You need a connection to install a Skin, and after that the book and its Skin work offline.
