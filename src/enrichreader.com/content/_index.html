Character profiles that follow your progress

Illustrative profile examples from Mary Shelley's Frankenstein (1831 text), with spoilers through chapter 24, the last chapter. These are rewritten examples, not quotations from the novel. Scroll to see a profile change at different reading positions.

Walton's Letters (Letter 4)

Walton's ship is stuck in the Arctic ice when the crew sees a dog sledge racing north. Its driver, the traveller, has the shape of a man but looks far too big. In Letter 4, nobody on board knows who he is.

Identity Unknown Sledge Driver Heading North

A huge figure seen from Walton's ship, which is stuck in the Arctic ice. In Letter 4, he drives a dog sledge north until the crew loses sight of him. Nobody on board knows who or what he is.

The Cottage (Ch. 12)

At night he gathers firewood for the poor family next door, and they never see who helps them. In Chapter 12, the Creature learns his first words by listening through a gap in the wall.

Entity: Character
The Creature
Victor's Creation Learning to Speak Secret Helper

Victor's creation, telling his own story. In Chapter 12, he hides in a shed against a poor family's cottage, gathers their firewood in secret, and learns words by listening. Then he sees his reflection in a pool.

The Arctic (Ch. 24) (Major Spoiler!)

Victor lies dead in Walton's cabin, and a giant figure stands over the body. In Chapter 24, the sledge driver from Letter 4 turns out to be the Creature, who mourns his maker.

Entity: Character
The Creature
Killer of Three The Sledge Traveller Full of Remorse

The sledge driver from Letter 4. He killed William, Clerval and Elizabeth, then led Victor north. In Chapter 24, he grieves over Victor's body, says he will burn himself, and drifts away on an ice raft.

How EnrichReader Works

Three steps to turn your static books into a living, interactive world. No lock-in, just better reading.

See how it works, in detail
1

Your book stays on your device

Import and read your book on your device. Optional sync stores library details and reading positions; reporting a chapter problem sends that chapter’s text. See the privacy policy for data controls.

2

The Skin

Sign in to find and install a matching Skin for a supported story. Skins are prepared in advance and add character profiles and lore lookup.

3

The Experience

With a matching Skin installed, character profiles and lore follow your reading progress as you turn the pages.

From static pages
to a living story

We build living archives for long-running fiction to keep you immersed without interruption.

01/

Character Profile

With a matching Skin, open a character profile limited to your reading progress. Chapter references show where its facts come from.

02/

The Codex

Unlock a living archive of plots, characters, items, and locations as you progress. The story reveals its secrets layer by layer.

03/

Atmosphere

Weather and light behind the text, and artwork at key scenes, tied to your reading progress. Availability depends on the Skin, and you can turn atmosphere off in settings.
