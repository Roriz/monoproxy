01 When you forget a name

Look up a character
without reading ahead.

Long LitRPG and progression fantasy series can run for hundreds of chapters across years of releases. Characters pick up new names, classes and titles, minor faces return arcs later, and a pause between books means relearning who belongs to which faction.

A wiki page can give away a character's fate before you've finished the book. With a matching guide or Skin, EnrichReader limits what you see to your reading position. If you're returning after a break, check your saved position first, then look up the people, places and lore you've already encountered.

03 Books, accounts and privacy

Before you start.

You'll need your own book, and a matching guide or Skin for companion features.

Current reader access
Reading, Skins and sync are currently free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers, as set out in the current reader terms.
Check your story
Check browser guides and the app's Skin catalog separately. A published guide doesn't guarantee an app Skin for the same book.
Bring your book
You'll need your own lawfully obtained copy of the novel. The reader opens DRM-free book files; EnrichReader doesn't provide the books.
Reading & accounts
You can read your books without an account. Sign in to install Skins or sync your library. Before you go offline, download your book and any Skin you need.
Your book stays on your device
The app stores imported books on your device. If you report a chapter problem, the report includes that chapter's text. The privacy policy explains sync, usage data and the settings you can change.

04 Questions from LitRPG readers

LitRPG companion FAQ.

Which LitRPG and web novel series are supported?

Those with a Skin in the app's catalog. Coverage isn't universal and changes over time, so check the catalog for your series and edition. If yours is missing, request a companion in the app; requests have no promised delivery date.

Can I use it for a story I read on Royal Road or another serial site?

The app reads book files you have, in EPUB, PDF or TXT without DRM. It doesn't connect to serial websites. A browser guide works beside any reader, but guides exist only for the books listed on the reading guides page.

I took a break between books. Can it catch me up?

With a matching Skin, open the book at your saved position. The Codex flags what's new since your last session, and when the Skin includes story data, a briefing recaps the current arc and the leads still open.

Coming back after a break ↗

Can I look up a character I haven't met yet?

Search only finds people you've already run into, and the app asks before showing anything past your position. To see more, loosen the spoiler shield to the end of your volume, or switch it off for a reread.

How is this different from a fan wiki?

A wiki is written from the latest chapter and covers far more series. The companion covers fewer stories, but it stops at your position and sits inside the reader, so you stay on the page.

What if a profile is wrong?

Companion data can contain errors, including unintended spoilers. Every profile has a report option. A report includes that chapter's text so the mistake can be checked and fixed in the Skin.
