# EnrichReader crawl rules.

# Public pages are open to search engines and to AI search, assistant and training crawlers.

# Spoiler boundary: reading-guide URLs that carry a reading position (?l= book, ?c= chapter) show
# story details up to that point. They answer "noindex, nofollow" (HTML and X-Robots-Tag) and
# point their canonical at the clean guide URL. Search engines may fetch them to see that. AI
# crawlers, which don't all honour noindex, are asked not to fetch them at all. A group below
# replaces the "*" group for the crawlers it names, so each group repeats every rule.
