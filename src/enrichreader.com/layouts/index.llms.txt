# EnrichReader

> {{ site.Params.description }}

The pages below are in English. The home page and the reader pages also have Brazilian Portuguese versions at the same path under {{ site.BaseURL }}pt-br/ (for example {{ site.BaseURL }}pt-br/story-companion/), linked from each page as an hreflang alternate. Other pages can be switched to Portuguese in the browser.

Story details in EnrichReader depend on how far the reader has got. Reading guides only show characters and context once the reader picks a position, and guide pages for a specific position are kept out of search on purpose, as are the series statistics reports. Please don't treat them as general summaries of a story.
