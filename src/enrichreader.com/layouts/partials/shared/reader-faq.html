Questions about EnrichReader

What is EnrichReader?

EnrichReader is a reading app for long stories. With a matching Skin, character profiles and lore follow your reading progress. Atmosphere effects are available in supported Skins.

Which platforms are supported?

EnrichReader runs as an Android app and as a web reader in any modern browser. On iPhone and iPad, open the web reader and add it to your Home Screen. Some books also have a reading guide on our website, but the app gives you the full experience: your book and its companion in one place, even offline once you download them.

Which book formats can I import?

You can import EPUB, PDF and TXT files that you obtained lawfully and that have no DRM. Compatibility can vary by edition and PDF layout. Skins are separate companion data, so you still need your own copy of the book.

How is it different from a fandom wiki?

A wiki article can reveal a character’s whole story at once. With a matching Skin, EnrichReader limits character profiles, lore, and relevant artwork to your reading position.

Which series are supported?

Check the in-app Skin catalog for your story and edition. Ordinary reading works without a Skin, but companion features need a match. You can request a missing companion in the app; requests have no promised delivery date.

Does it work offline? Are my books uploaded?

Yes. Your books are stored on your device. Install the app and download the book and any Skin before you go offline. Ordinary reading and sync do not upload book text. If you submit a chapter-problem report, it includes that chapter’s text. See our privacy policy for details.

Is EnrichReader free?

Reading, Skins and sync are currently free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers. Current reader terms.

Do I need an account?

No. You can read your own books without an account. You only need one to install Skins and to sync.

Can it remind me who everyone is after a break?

With a matching Skin installed, open character profiles and the lore directory at your saved reading position to refresh your memory. Information is limited to that position, so check it is correct before looking up a character.
