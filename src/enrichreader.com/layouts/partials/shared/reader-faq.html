Questions about EnrichReader

What is EnrichReader?

EnrichReader is a reading app for long stories, such as webnovels and LitRPG series. You read your own copy of the book in the app. With a matching Skin for a supported story, you can tap a character’s name to see a profile limited to the chapters you’ve read, and some Skins add atmosphere. It runs on Android and in the web browser, and reader access is currently free.

What is a Skin?

A Skin is the companion for one story: its characters, places and lore, each fact tagged with the chapter that reveals it. Skins are prepared in advance and are separate from the book. Without a Skin, you can still read your book normally, just without profiles or atmosphere. See how Skins work for details.

Can I look up a character without seeing later spoilers?

Yes, in supported stories. With a matching Skin, a character profile shows only facts from chapters up to your reading position, and each fact names the chapter it comes from. Companion data can contain mistakes, so every profile has a way to report one. Without the app, a browser reading guide does the same for the books it covers once you choose your chapter.

How is it different from a fandom wiki?

A wiki article can reveal a character’s whole story at once. With a matching Skin, EnrichReader limits character profiles, lore, and relevant artwork to your reading position. A wiki usually covers more series and more detail. The spoiler-free wiki alternative guide compares the options side by side.

Is my series supported?

It depends on whether a Skin exists for your story and edition. Check the in-app Skin catalog. Ordinary reading works without a Skin, but companion features need a match. You can request a missing companion in the app; requests have no promised delivery date.

Do I need my own copy of the book?

Yes. EnrichReader does not sell or supply books. You import a book file you already have, and the Skin adds the companion on top of it.

Which book formats can I import?

EPUB, PDF and TXT files that you obtained lawfully and that have no DRM. Compatibility can vary by edition and PDF layout, and a Skin only lines up with editions close enough to the one it was prepared from.

Which platforms are supported?

EnrichReader runs as an Android app and as a web reader in any modern browser. On iPhone and iPad, open the web reader in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. Some books also have a reading guide on our website, but the app gives you the full experience: your book and its companion in one place.

Is EnrichReader free?

Yes, currently. Reading, Skins and sync are free, with no reader paywalls or paid feature tiers, as set out in the current reader terms.

Do I need an account?

No. You can read your own books without an account. You only need one to install Skins and to sync.

Does EnrichReader work offline?

Yes, once your book and its Skin are on your device. Install the app and download the book and any Skin before you go offline. Installing a Skin needs a connection.

Are my books uploaded?

No. Your books are stored on your device, and ordinary reading and sync do not upload book text. If you submit a chapter-problem report, it includes that chapter’s text. See our privacy policy for details.

Can it remind me who everyone is after a break?

With a matching Skin installed, open character profiles and the Codex at your saved reading position to refresh your memory. When the Skin has story data, a welcome-back briefing also recaps where you are and what was going on. Information is limited to that position, so check it is correct before looking up a character. For more on picking a series back up, see reading with a story companion.
