If you are working in src/enrichreader.com/content/enterprise pages or a landing page focused in B2B, read docs/b2b_playbook.md to follow the best practices.
Before writing or editing any public EnrichReader copy (src/enrichreader.com pages and translations, the web guide, Play Store listing, social or outreach text), read the "Copy rules" section of docs/enrichreader-seo.md. In short: never name a file format such as EPUB; never say "web reader" (lead with the native app); always say book content never reaches our servers and is never shared, and never describe the chapter report payload.
